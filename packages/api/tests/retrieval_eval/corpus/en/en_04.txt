WAREHOUSE LEASE AGREEMENT

This lease is made between {{PII:ORGANIZATION_NAME:1}}, the landlord, represented by {{PII:PERSON_NAME:1}}, and {{PII:ORGANIZATION_NAME:2}}, the tenant, represented by {{PII:PERSON_NAME:2}}. The leased premises are located at {{PII:LOCATION:1}}.

Article 1. Premises

1.1 The landlord leases to the tenant a warehouse hall of about two thousand four hundred square metres, together with an office mezzanine of ninety square metres and eight parking spaces in the yard.

1.2 Three loading docks with levellers, one ground-level roller door and the fire alarm system belong to the premises. The tenant receives all keys and access cards on the handover date and signs a handover report.

Article 2. Term

2.1 The lease starts on the handover date and runs for five years. The tenant has the option to extend it once, for a further three years, by written notice given no later than nine months before expiry.

2.2 During the first twelve months neither party may terminate the lease for convenience.

Article 3. Rent and Charges

3.1 The monthly base rent is payable in advance by the fifth day of each month to the landlord's account with IBAN {{PII:IBAN:1}}.

3.2 Service charges for building insurance, property tax, yard lighting and snow clearing are paid as monthly advances and settled once a year. Electricity and water are metered separately and billed to the tenant at cost.

3.3 The rent is adjusted every year on the anniversary of the start date according to the change in the consumer price index. A decrease in the index does not reduce the rent below the amount agreed at signing.

3.4 The tenant paid a deposit equal to three months' base rent. The landlord holds it in a separate account and returns it, with interest, within two months after the premises are handed back in proper condition.

Article 4. Use of the Premises

4.1 The tenant may use the premises only for storage, packing and dispatch of non-hazardous goods. Storing flammable liquids, gas cylinders or chemicals requires the landlord's written consent and proof of additional insurance.

4.2 The maximum floor load is five tonnes per square metre. Racking higher than eight metres must be approved by a structural engineer at the tenant's cost.

4.3 Operating hours are between 05:00 and 22:00 on weekdays and 07:00 and 18:00 on Saturdays. Work on Sundays and public holidays needs the landlord's consent because of noise limits in the surrounding district.

Article 5. Maintenance and Repairs

5.1 The landlord is responsible for roof, walls, structure, heating system and the external fire protection equipment.

5.2 The tenant carries out minor repairs, up to a cost of five hundred euros per case, for items such as lighting, door fittings, dock seals and floor markings. The tenant keeps the yard clean and free of waste.

5.3 The tenant must inform the landlord immediately of any damage that could endanger people or the building, including water ingress and cracks in the floor slab.

Article 6. Alterations and Subletting

6.1 The tenant may not make structural alterations without written consent. Non-structural installations, such as shelving, partition walls and cable trays, are allowed and must be removed at the end of the lease unless the parties agree otherwise.

6.2 Subletting part of the premises to a third party requires the landlord's prior consent, which may not be unreasonably withheld for a sublessee in a comparable line of business.

Article 7. Insurance and Liability

7.1 The tenant insures its stock and equipment against fire, water damage and theft, and delivers the policy to the landlord on request.

7.2 The landlord is not liable for loss of goods unless the loss results from its intent or gross negligence.

Article 8. Return of the Premises

8.1 At the end of the lease the tenant returns the premises broom-clean and in the condition recorded in the handover report, except for normal wear and tear. A joint inspection takes place at least two weeks before the end date, and the landlord can call {{PII:PHONE_NUMBER:1}} to arrange it.

8.2 Costs of remedying damage that goes beyond normal wear and tear are deducted from the deposit.

Article 9. Final Provisions

9.1 Amendments must be in writing. If a provision is invalid, the remaining provisions stay in force and the parties replace the invalid one with a valid provision that comes closest to its purpose.
